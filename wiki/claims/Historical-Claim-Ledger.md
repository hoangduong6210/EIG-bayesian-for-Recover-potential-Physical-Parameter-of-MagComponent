# Historical Claim Ledger

The validated release `20260817T072230Z_401e3030fe13` supersedes preliminary
interpretations that could imply general EIG superiority. The current record
retains the fixed-traversal improvement only beside the ties and cost-aware
loss against strong comparators. Future gate-aligned utilities are new
experiments and cannot be substituted into this release.

MM-3 subsequently replaced “structural model mismatch remains unevaluated”
with a narrower negative result: under three preregistered synthetic
departures, the local precision gate can become false-confident. It did not
convert that result into a claim of measured-material robustness or general
policy superiority. [Source E16](../evidence/Evidence-Sources.md#e16)
