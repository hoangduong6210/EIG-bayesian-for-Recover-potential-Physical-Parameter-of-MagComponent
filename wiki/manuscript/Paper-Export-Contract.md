# Paper Export Contract

A paper may use only reviewed, admitted claim language with resolved evidence
and limitations. The existing build pins release ID and manifest digest,
projection and figure hashes, bibliography, toolchain, and manuscript inputs.
A named export also records the source wiki commit and claim set. A later
result creates a new snapshot rather than changing an archived one.
