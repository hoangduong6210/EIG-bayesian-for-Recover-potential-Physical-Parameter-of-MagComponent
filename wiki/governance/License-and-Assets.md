# License and Asset Boundaries

Measured-data licenses, bibliography records, generated figures, and public
evidence projections retain distinct provenance. Production records containing
operational metadata are not publication assets. An export must verify figure
hashes, bibliography completeness, source terms, and disclosure boundaries.

The document exporter bundles the unmodified `IEEEtran.bst` version 1.14
(2015-08-26), distributed by Michael Shell under the LaTeX Project Public
License 1.3. Its original notices remain in the file. The source is the
[IEEEtran distribution on CTAN](https://ctan.org/pkg/ieeetran).
The bibliography uses numbered references in citation order; reference
formatting follows the [IEEE Author Center guidance](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/your-role-in-article-production/ieee-editorial-style-manual/).
