# Research Workflow

1. Freeze model, candidate library, truth generation, costs, gates, policies,
   seeds, and estimator qualification.
2. Preserve per-state diagnostics and per-seed policy trajectories.
3. Reconstruct counts, costs, gate status, holdout values, and parameters.
4. Require exact seed, policy, contrast, artifact, and hash coverage.
5. Generate a disclosure-safe projection from the verified release.
6. Admit scoped claims only after evidence review.
7. Export a versioned paper snapshot from the admitted claim set.
